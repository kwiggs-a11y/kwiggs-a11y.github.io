How far below a previous high, and for how long

Read these two figures together. The chart above is built from 251 monthly returns; the -38.30% figure is computed from 5,246 daily returns between 2005-02-24 and 2025-12-30. Monthly data smooths over declines that begin and end inside one month, so the curve bottoms out shallower than the true measured low. The daily figure is the honest one, and it is the one we publish. Annualized volatility over the full series is 14.15%.
